Accursed

Half-Caster - I Wish It Was Just a Phase

Main Stat(s): i forgor

The Accursed are, well, cursed. They don't let that keep them down. Why cry about your curse when you can make it your own - control it so it doesn't control you.

Barbarian

Martial - Too Angry to Die

Main Stat(s): Constitution

Barbarians perform ancient battle rites which involve loosing oneself to a terrible rage. These traditions are no secrets, but are too harrowing for most to consider following them. Certain ancestral practices of Barbarians lets them tap into primal magic.

Bard

Caster - The Power of Song

Main Stat(s): Charisma

Bards are well versed in many disciplines, often called jacks of all trades. Bards, traditionally, are lone travelling storytellers, shamans, teachers, messengers, and artists. They channel arcane magics by the spread of knowledge, through the form of music. It is unclear, even to the gods, how the magic of Bards came to be. Bards are born randomly, unknowingly, but are always drawn towards the principles of bardship and the arts.

Bender

Half-Caster - Earth Wind and Fire

Main Stat(s): i forgor

Water, Earth, Fire, Air. Long ago, there was a popular show about that I think. Anyways, the Bender draws upon the chaotic nature of the elemental planes that touch all of reality. They can sepcialize in one element, or dabble in multiple. They utilize martial arts to guide and shape their elemental power.

Cleric

Caster - Stewards of Divinity

Main Stat(s): i forgor

Clerics are devoted servants of the divine. Through stewardship of higher powers, they become part of their god's Domain, and an extension of their influence. A Cleric's Domain determines what spells they cast and why they cast them. Many Clerics worship a specific god, but some follow just the concepts of certain Domains.

Druid

Caster - Stewards of Nature

Main Stat(s): Wisdom

Druids are protectors of nature, scorning the unchecked damage of wars and growth of cities. They are part of secret societies called Circles. Their magic is taught to them by nature spirits in their Circle. Druids physically and magically embody aspects of nature.

Fighter

Martial - By the Sword

Main Stat(s): Strength or Dexterity

Fighters are those who have devoted themselves to attaining the peak of combat skill - they are no mere soldiers. Combat is like an art to them, and how they weild their weapons would make doubters believe them. There are many paths a Fighter can walk to greatness, two are rarely the same.

Gunslinger

Martial - Eat Lead

Main Stat(s): Dexterity

Some may find a Gunslinger out of place in a fantasy setting. You are wrong, knights used guns (real btw). Rootin' and Shootin' their way through problems, the Gunslinger uses the not-so-cutting-edge and perhaps-not-entirely-safe arts of magical engineering and black powder to fire hot slag at distant foes.

Inventor

Half-Caster - Mad Scientist? Huh? I'm Not Angry?

Main Stat(s): Intelligence

Want to make your own magic items, constructs to fight for you, or maybe a magic canon? Tired of long magic item crafting times and high cost? Do you like steampunk? Well the Inventor has got you covered! This gadgeteering class is all about customization.

Occultist

Caster - Weird Magic

Main Stat(s): AAA

Occultists research a particular branch of unusual magic. Perhaps it is magic lost to time, banned, looked down upon, or convention breaking. They are not dark mages, but sometimes it gets hard to tell.

Paladin

Half-Caster - Sworn to the Ideal

Main Stat(s): Strength and Charisma

Paladins are oathsworn warriors known for their magic that stems from their oath. Clans of paladins form around the same oath. Paladins must follow their oath, even into death, or loose their power and be shunned. They adventure to follow the tenets of their oath, seeking to banish what their oath sees as the ultimate evil. Though their magic is divine in nature, it does not actually come from following a god. Their tenets often lead them to face their foes head on, ready to fell them with their divine smite.

Psion

Caster - Big Brain Time

Main Stat(s): Intelligence

Psions are all about psychic abilities, like telepathy, telekinesis, transposition, psychokinetics, and more. Their minds have been altered, perhaps by enlightenment, or beholding an incomprehensible outer god, or their emotions are so powerful they twist reality.

Pugilist

Martial - Tavern Brawler Extraordinaire

Main Stat(s): Strength and Constitution

What can go wrong when you bring nothing but your fists to a sword and magic fight? Not much, says the Pugilist, an expert in hand-to-anything combat. Pesky wizard in your way? Give him free chiropractic treatment! Warrior in plate armor? Ignore the knuckle breaking sounds and flip them over!

Ranger

Half-Caster - 100% Free Rnage Warrior

Main Stat(s): Dexterity and Wisdom

Rangers are versatile warriors who live in the wilds, usually in small, secretive groups called Packs. They are skilled with weapons, especially the bow. Through secret rites, Rangers obtain unique magics related to nature. Rangers tend to be nomads, experts in survival and hunting - though not in the usual sense. Each Pack is dedicated to hunting a certain kind of threat, which ranges from rampaging beasts, to scheming criminals, to great dragons.

Rogue

Martial - Sneaky Little Bugger

Main Stat(s): Dexterity

Rogues are those who practice fighting dirty, spend their time in the underbelly of cities, and work outside the law. Many rogues are occupational theives, assassins, scammers, and spies. A good rogue is cunning, quick, and deadly... maybe even charming. All rogues work in the dark, whether its for their own gain, or so others can stay in the light.

Savant (Monk)

Martial - Legally Distinct Kamehameha

Main Stat(s): Dexterity and Wisdom

Savants harness the power of their very souls to aid them. They dedicate their lives to training to perfection. It is said that their dedication causes their chosen weapon to become an extension of their soul. They manipulate their core to create magical effects without casting spells.

Scholar

Martial - The Pen is Mightier

Main Stat(s): Intelligence

The Scholar took the saying Brains over Brawn a little too seriously. A Scholar, is quite literally, a scholar, who, like the wizard, has taken their study to the field. A very dangerous field. They aren't worried however, they may not be magical, but they got their wits!

Shaman

Caster - I See Dead People

Main Stat(s): i forgor

Shamans act as a bridge between the Material Plane, the astral realm, and realms of the afterlife, communing with spirits and ghosts. They usually live as aesthetics, medicinemen, and monks. They channel the power of spirits they commune with to cast spells.

Sorcerer

Caster - Nepotis- I Mean Bloodline Magic

Main Stat(s): Charisma

A Sorcerer's bloodline is the source of their magic. They are born from an ancestor who may have been something like a god, dragon, or legendary mortal. The families of Sorcerers tend to form clans with a lot of social influence. Their physical bodies have a direct connection to the Weave, allowing them to modify spells as they cast them.

Vanguard

Martial - Can't Stop the Wall

Main Stat(s): Strength and Constitution

All this sword and board is cool and all but I WANT A REALLY BIG SHIELD! The Vanguard is all about tanking and protecting allies, especially with the aforementioned REALLY BIG SHIELD. May not be indestructible, but its close enough.

Warlock

Caster - Read the Fine Print

Main Stat(s): Charisma

A Warlock's magic is given to them by an especially powerful being through a contract. The circumstances of a Warlock vary greatly from contract to contract. Some are tricked into it, some seek power, some make a wish in exchange for servitude. Their magic is limited, but always strong.

Weaver

Half-Caster - God's Favorite

Main Stat(s): ???

The Weaver is an incredibly rare class, with the number of Weavers in a world numbering in the single digits. Their existence is a mystery but they always seem to be in the center of history. A Weaver can physically sense and interact with the Weave directly. They pull and shape the threads of the Weave itself to literally craft spells. Each Weaver have found a different kind of item intrinsic to them they can weave their spells onto.

Witch

Caster - Broomstick Baddie

Main Stat(s): i forgor

Witches are those who have been cursed by the Weave itself with the Witches' Curse, chosen to suffer, twisted by the magic within them. Through force of personality and grueling effort, they can reflect their curse onto the world around them. They are stigmatized by society for the curse and often hunted.

Wizard

Caster - Knowledge is (Arcane) Power

Main Stat(s): Intelligence

Wizards are devoted to studying magic, constantly in search of new spells and greater understanding. They carry a spellbook in which they hold their spells. A wizard will specialize is a certain school of magic. Through their magic, a Wizard may try to make some kind of breakthrough, perhaps inventing a new spell for a special purpose. Their study will often lead them into the most dangerous situations.
